# User Flow: Loan & Credit Card Discovery

## Overview
Allows authenticated users to inspect their credit eligibility score, browse pre-approved loan amounts, and evaluate curated credit card options.

## Steps
1. **Home Feed** (`credbuddha-discovery-home-feed-default-01.jpg`): Shows real-time credit score meter and recommended loan products.
2. **Pre-Approved Offers** (`credbuddha-discovery-preapproved-offers-default-02.jpg`): Displays tiered lender options with fixed interest rates and EMI calculators.
3. **Credit Card Hub** (`credbuddha-discovery-creditcard-promo-default-03.jpg`): Compares cards with cashback, travel rewards, and zero annual fee offers.
